Airliner models in High 3D quality: amvlab 3d Aircraft Models, CC BY 4.0, used as published (scaled to each type's real length). 3D engine: three.js (MIT).

Free, open-source Piper voices that run in the game (they use more battery and make the device warmer than its own voices). Over a thousand pilot voices, with accents. Each device downloads them once (about 170 to 260 MB per airport, depending on its airlines), then they work offline. Without them the device's own voices are used.

An open-source speech model (OpenAI Whisper) that runs on this device instead of the browser's own recogniser. It is told the callsigns on frequency, this airport's taxiways and the usual phrases, and what it hears is matched to them by sound, so callsigns that aren't English words (Shenzhen, Loong Air, Speedbird...) are found. About 80 MB, downloaded once, then it works offline. Until it is ready the browser's recogniser is used. Recognition takes a moment after you stop talking.

Tap Start talking, speak, then tap Stop and send (it also sends by itself after a pause). Like a real radio, the other aircraft are muted while you talk and are heard again when you let go. Tap an aircraft and you can leave out the callsign. On a PC you can also hold the space bar.
