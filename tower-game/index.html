Say the callsign, then the instruction. Examples:
Speedbird 123, runway 27, cleared to land
Air China 1234, pushback approved
Air China 1234, taxi to holding point runway 27 via alpha
Delta 45, cross runway 15 · hold position · continue taxi
Delta 45, runway 27, line up and wait · cleared for take-off
Delta 45, contact departure, good day · go around · hold short runway 27
Tap an aircraft first and you can leave out the callsign. On a PC, hold the space bar to talk.
